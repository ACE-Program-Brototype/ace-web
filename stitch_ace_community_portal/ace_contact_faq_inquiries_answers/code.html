Frequently Asked Questions

Eligibility is reserved for candidates who demonstrate exceptional aptitude and a documented history of high performance in their respective fields. Specific prerequisites vary by cohort, but generally require prior foundational experience.
The process is multi-staged, beginning with an initial dossier review, followed by technical assessments, and concluding with a series of interviews with our senior Handlers to ensure cultural and academic alignment.
Monthly reviews are comprehensive evaluations of project deliverables, conceptual grasp, and peer collaboration. They are conducted in a board-style format where candidates defend their methodologies to a panel.
Cross-batch transfers are considered on a case-by-case basis and require endorsement from your current primary Handler along with a formal portfolio submission.
